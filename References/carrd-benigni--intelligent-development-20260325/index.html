MEET

Carlo Benigni

Director & Founder

Carlo has extensive experience and proven track record in the planning, design and development delivery of iconic, large scale commercial and mixed-use projects in Central London. He is a proven specialist in establishing value enhancement and design optimisation to maximise project value collaboratively with globally recognised consultant teams.Prior to establishing Benigni he was Deputy Design Director at Brookfield Properties, UK where he led the design and development process, resulting in the establishment of a £2.5b and 2.5m sq. ft. portfolio of commercial and mixed-use assets in the City of London. Before that he worked at SOM, London, a leading international architecture firm.Carlo holds a Bachelor of Science in Architecture and Engineering from University of Westminster, a Postgraduate Diploma in Architecture from The Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL. He is a RIBA Chartered Architect, licensed in the UK (ARB) and Sweden (SAR/MSA), a LEED Accredited Professional® and a Liveryman of the Worshipful Company of Chartered Architects in the City of London.

INFO

Creating A New Community

A 14-acre mixed-use masterplan re-development providing a new cultural and entertainment led destination in West London, whilst preserving the 135-year-old legacy of the historical Olympia Exhibition halls. The new development will include 40,000 sq.ft. of f&b retail, 2.5-acres landscaped public realm, 1500-seat Theatre, a 3,500-seat music venue, 145-keys boutique hotel, 195-keys international hotel, 550,000 sq.ft. of A-grade offices, 30,000 sq.ft. high end restaurant / live performance venue, 4-screen art house cinema, 80,000 sq.ft. creative studios, 23,000 sq.ft. exhibition space. Pre-lettings have been agreed with AEG (music venue), Hyatt (international hotel) and CitizenM (boutique hotel).Benigni Ltd provided a range of Design and Development advisory services, managing the client design process for the whole masterplan, leading value enhancement initiatives, cost control and working on a number of lease agreements for occupiers in the office, entertainment, education and hospitality sector.

WHAT ARE COOKIES?
Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device by websites that you visit. They are widely used in order to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.The cookies used by us are based on the International Chamber of Commerce guide for cookie categories:Strictly necessary
Performance
Functionality
TargetingWHAT DO COOKIES DO FOR ME?
‘Strictly necessary’ cookies are essential in order to enable you to move around our website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas of our website (eg My Account). Without these essential cookies, services you have specifically asked for cannot be provided, such as asking for a quote or applying for one of our apartments.‘Performance cookies’ collect information about how you use our website eg which pages you visit, and if you experience any errors. These cookies don’t collect any information that could identify you – all the information collected is anonymous and is used to help us improve how our website works.‘Functionality’ cookies are used to provide other services such as our online calculators and certain navigation elements of the website which enhance your browsing experience, for example, directing you back to a promotion that might have attracted your attention on your first visit. They can also enable us to remember setting choices you make to improve your visit.‘Targeting’ cookies are used to collect information in order to deliver adverts more relevant to you and your interests. These cookies also allow you to ‘like’ and ‘share’ our pages via social networking sites such as Facebook.We do not currently use first party targeted cookies on this website.WHAT DO I NEED TO DO?
The cookie settings on this website are set to ‘allow all cookies’ to improve the overall performance of our website and give you the best user experience.If you click on ‘OK’, you consent to this and you do not need to do anything further.
