Overview

Unlocking hidden value

We are a purpose-driven company that seamlessly integrates design and development expertise to create extraordinary places where people can thrive.

With over 20 years of experience, we lead with an unwavering focus on innovation, sustainability, and community engagement. Our comprehensive development solutions maximise stakeholder value, delivering unique places that offer real, lasting worth for all.

What We Do

We help investors optimise the development process to unlock real estate value

We have extensive experience managing the design and development process at all stages of the development cycle. We love getting involved from the outset of a project and are comfortable getting parachuted in at any stage.

01

Design & Development Manager

We specialise in delivering exceptional real estate projects for landowners and third-party investors. Our comprehensive approach is designed to maximise value and financial returns while ensuring the successful realisation of major development projects — from establishing a visionary development brief through to completion.

02

Strategic Advisor

We offer strategic advice to major landowners, both public and private, on developing strategic visions and optimal delivery models. Our process typically involves a thorough, in-depth review of existing proposals to ensure the best possible outcomes for all stakeholders.

03

Fractional Development Director

We serve as Development Director for businesses that prefer flexible, on-demand expertise over hiring a full-time resource. We provide tailored development management precisely when you need it, ensuring your projects receive expert guidance without the commitment of permanent staff.

Service Toolkit

A service offer to help at any stage of the development cycle

We provide strategic integrated design and development advice to investors and asset owners during the origination, planning, design, and development delivery of projects.

We are specialists in establishing value enhancement and design optimisation to maximise project value in collaboration with our partners, the local community, high-profile architects, and leading professional advisors.

We work on complex commercial and mixed-use schemes, ranging from ground-up redevelopments to the repurposing and refurbishment of real estate assets.

We service the full development cycle as well as providing targeted advice on specific project phases.

People

Carlo Benigni
Director & Founder

Carlo has extensive experience and a proven track record in the planning, design, and development delivery of iconic, large-scale commercial and mixed-use projects in Central London. He is a proven specialist in establishing value enhancement and design optimisation to maximise project value collaboratively with globally recognised consultant teams.

Prior to establishing Benigni, Carlo served as Deputy Design Director at Brookfield Properties UK, where he led the design and development process — resulting in the establishment of a £2.5 billion, 2.5 million sq.ft. portfolio of commercial and mixed-use assets in the City of London. Before that, he worked at SOM London, a leading international architecture firm.

We believe in open and collaborative relationships. We are able to set up quickly and work with a selected network of like-minded independent professional partners to deliver an exceptional service.
